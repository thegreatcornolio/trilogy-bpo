The challenge

Traditional BPO savings stop at labour arbitrage.

Providers earn margin on headcount. When automation reduces staff, it reduces their income — a powerful disincentive to innovate. Bottom line: if a supplier makes less money when you automate, they will never automate fast enough.

The DBIT methodology

The DBIT Methodology — Design, Build, Innovate & Transfer

A phased, holistic build — not a facilities fit-out on its own. DBIT covers every layer of the operation so localisation risk is designed out, not discovered at go-live.

  1. 01Design
    We architect your operating model, technology stack and organisational structure around your specific service-delivery requirements and growth trajectory.
    Then
  2. 02Build
    We stand up the full operation — recruitment, training, facilities, technology implementation, compliance and governance frameworks.
    Then
  3. 03Innovate
    We actively reduce required headcount through digital deflection, intelligent routing, automation and AI-assisted resolution — because we are not paid on heads.
    Then
  4. 04Transfer
    We transition full operational ownership to your team when you are ready, with ongoing support as needed.
